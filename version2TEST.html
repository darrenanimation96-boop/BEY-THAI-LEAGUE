Competition rules

Match System

Suggested competition flow from regular season to championship. Adjust it to the official rules.

Phase 01
Regular Season

Eight teams play a single round robin. Each team faces the other seven once: 28 games total.

Phase 02
Final Standings

Rank by wins, then use head-to-head and point differential as proposed tie-breakers.

Phase 03
Semifinals

Seed 1 plays Seed 4; Seed 2 plays Seed 3. Winners advance.

Phase 04
Championship

Semifinal winners play for the title. A third-place game is optional.
